Methodology

Seven weighted factors, each scored 0–10 against sector norms, blended into a single composite out of 100.

This mirrors how a mainboard desk actually triages a subscription decision: is the pricing fair against listed comparables, is the underlying business actually growing, is the book being filled by real demand or just retail FOMO, does the grey market agree, are the promoters selling out or building in, is the money raised going to work or into someone's pocket, and is the sector in favour right now. No single factor decides the call — the composite does.

Not investment advice. Scores are a structured read of publicly reported issue data, financials, subscription figures and grey‑market premium — refreshed daily, snapshot timestamped above. GMP is an unregulated, unofficial indicator and can move sharply right up to listing. Figures for issues still in their bidding window (subscription, GMP) will keep changing between refreshes; already‑listed issues show their realised listing‑day gain in place of GMP. Sector P/E comparisons are approximate peer‑set averages, not exact index figures. Always cross‑check the RHP, review your own risk appetite, and consult a SEBI‑registered adviser before applying.
